## Statistical interpretation

### Postcode 81245 · public sold references and period-matched rates

`src/data/localSales.ts` contains fourteen selected apartment references (including seven @home developer unit references) and five secondary house references from original pages marked sold, reviewed 2026-10-25. Apartments are the default. Four apartments and three houses disclose a listing price; none establishes a notarised closing price or a completion date. These are factual observations with original links and publisher/property identifiers, not a complete register of completed transactions. Neither the review date, crawl date, energy-certificate date, publication/update date, nor “available from” date is used as a sale date. Unknown tenure stays unknown; explicitly leasehold properties are marked separately.

`src/data/neupasing.ts` records the municipal ≈2,400-home Paul-Gerhardt-Allee quarter and ten named project/subset records. The municipal number includes rental housing and is not a total of apartment sale contracts. Original developer/contractor sources establish project counts: @home’s 131 marketed ownership apartments and explicit 100% sell-out; Belvedere’s 164 units handed to owners; PENTA’s 293 units; anders wohnen’s completed 373-unit mixed project; EASY’s 305; PRISMA’s 255; the roughly 450-home WA7 project; and GVG’s 240. The 295 figure adds only the two non-overlapping developer sold/handover reports; no other project’s constructed or unavailable homes are inferred to be sales. These are publisher reports, not an official transaction register.

Münchner Wohnen’s 17 May 2024 acquisition announcement describes 68 homes in three buildings acquired from INDUWO. Its 12 May 2026 update confirms handover and distinguishes a further 54 homes from its own construction. The 68 homes represent one institutional block purchase, not 68 separate retail transactions. Neither the 122 combined homes, parent project totals, nor individual unit samples are added again. No complete coverage percentage is calculated from mixed project/subset counts. Seven @home unit examples overlap its 131 sold apartments, which is explicit in each row and export. The project is in the Paul-Gerhardt-Allee quarter; marketing labels such as Obermenzing are not treated as precise neighborhood boundaries.

Three project-specific asking-price ranges from Fischer Immobilien’s contemporary 19 September 2017 review are retained with the original article, publication date and asking-price basis. They are not verified closing prices. ECB rates are matched only to that publication month, not to an unknown sale date. No growth index or neighborhood resale mean is inferred. The wider citywide Munich apartment means remain explicitly new-build-only.

Apartment records retain living area, rooms, floor, Baujahr, Hausgeld and separately priced parking where the original source discloses them. Hausgeld is a listing observation with its accounting period or an explicit unknown period, not a current WEG budget. Fischer 187 quotes 2006 accounts and a separate €88 reserve contribution; its price placeholder of zero is stored as undisclosed. Dürrenberger 149 has conflicting Baujahr statements (2015/2017), so no construction year is selected. Dürrenberger 158 explicitly lists €343.58 monthly ground rent, a lease ending in 2086 and a 65% maximum mortgage charge; those are source terms, not lender approval. Immosmart FH-SIM14 retains area/rooms/year from the original page’s structured property data; no Hausgeld from stale search snippets is used. Displayed €/m² divides the listed property price by advertised living area and excludes extra parking; terrace allowances and leasehold make direct comparisons imperfect. No average of these heterogeneous asking prices is represented as a closing-price benchmark.

The sample spans postcode 81245, including publisher labels for Pasing, Obermenzing and Aubing and a separately selectable Paul-Gerhardt-Allee project scope. No precise Neupasing polygon has been applied to individual references. A mixed Aubing/Pasing source label is kept unresolved. An empty filtered sample never establishes zero actual sales. Cross-publisher duplicates cannot be fully ruled out from undisclosed addresses. No average closing-price estimate is derived from these references.

The property facts, original canonical links and review dates are maintained manually. Sources include Dürrenberger, Weyland, Hegerich, Immosmart, Fischer, Daniela Mauritz and ADAMI; each observation links directly to its original. Mutable list-cursor URLs are excluded because they can resolve to a different property later. No photos, broker descriptions or contact details are redistributed; original publisher rights remain applicable. Check originals before using an observation. Source disappearances and price/status changes require review.

Historical mortgage comparisons use ECB monthly AAR/NDER observations from January 2003, separated by initial fixation category. A period average is an arithmetic mean of available monthly observations, with available/expected month counts and partial-year disclosure. It is not loan-volume weighted, specific to 81245, or the financing rate paid by the referenced buyer. The Munich half-year table matches January–June rate observations to the same report period; municipal price means remain citywide and their segment counts remain undisclosed.

### NRW attribution conflicts

Reviewed 2026-10-25 against the [OpenPLZ NRW municipality directory](https://openplzapi.org/de/FederalStates/05/Municipalities?pageSize=50) (396 directory entries over eight pages). Five source codes carry a label inconsistent with that directory: `05974004` (Bad Sassendorf / Anröchte), `05974028` (Welver / Lippstadt), `05758032` (Vlotho / Spenge), `05758020` (Löhne / Kirchlengern), and `05758024` (Rödinghausen / Löhne). Neither the source name nor the code alone establishes which location the values belong to.

The UI withholds all reference records and historical summaries for those codes, without reassigning them. The original snapshot retains the published records for reproducibility. Of 386 source codes, 381 remain selectable. `src/data/nrwQuality.ts` holds the reviewed exclusions; any new duplicate municipality label is also withheld automatically. Review exclusions against corrected publisher files before removing them. A successful monthly import does not resolve an attribution conflict.

- An asking price is not a completed sale. Never mix them in a price distribution.
- An official land reference value does not include a home's building value. NRW **Immobilienrichtwerte** are a different product from **Bodenrichtwerte**.
- A median over reference zones is not a median over transactions. It is unweighted by area or sales volume, and changing coverage can move it even if underlying values do not change.
- NRW records may represent polygon fragments. The importer de-duplicates the same municipality, zone, type, object group, valuation date, value, build-year norm and area norm. Other reference attributes are not displayed; the original BORIS record must be checked for valuation use.
- Munich means change with the sample and property mix. The included typical living/plot sizes document part of that variation. A connected line is a visual summary of the reported observations, not a constant-quality index or proof of a specific home's change in value.
- The national Eurostat index describes broad transaction-price change and must never be transformed into invented local historical sale prices.
- Complete German address-level sale data is not publicly available as a uniform open feed. In Bavaria, the Kaufpreissammlung has access restrictions; detailed local reports/land values can require fees. The project makes the evidence boundary explicit rather than presenting estimates as records.

## Freshness and failures

An observation's period and a snapshot's retrieval date are different. Rate observations older than three calendar months are marked older in the UI. A retrieval today does not make an old observation current. Upstream publication schedules, revisions, API outages, rate limits and GitHub Actions inactivity can delay updates. Inspect Actions after a failure. Each importer validates its own source before replacing its file; a failed importer leaves that prior file intact. The workflow commits only after all selected refresh steps and tests succeed.

Manual sources require human review. Check Bavaria's report archive and the Munich report landing page at least quarterly, as well as state tax statutes when an announced change takes effect. Add new reports only after checking definitions and corrections. Do not assume a stable URL means the contents are unchanged.
